Help and support

We want JobBreak to be fair and easy. If anything about your purchase is not right, we will fix it.

What you get

Anyone can check a job and see a free preview of the result. The paid JobBreak Monitor plan unlocks your full breakdown and AI-researched plan. You can buy it once for a single full plan ($4.99), or subscribe to keep your outlook monitored ($1.99/month or $12/year).

I paid but I do not see my plan

Access is tied to the device and browser you bought on. If you cleared your cookies, switched browsers, or your checkout tab closed before it finished, you can restore access here. Paste the checkout session id from the confirmation page web address (the part after session_id=) or from your Stripe email receipt, then select Restore.

Cannot find it? Use the form at the bottom of this page with the email you paid with, and we will restore it for you.

Cancel a subscription

You can cancel the Monitor subscription any time and keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Paste your checkout session id below to open your secure Stripe billing page, where you can cancel, update your card, and download invoices.

A one-time $4.99 purchase is not a subscription, so there is nothing recurring to cancel.

Refunds

14-day, no-questions-asked refund. If you are not happy, ask within 14 days of your purchase and we will refund it in full. Use the form below with the email you paid with (and your checkout session id if you have it), choose Refund, and we will take care of it. Refunds return to your original payment method, usually within a few business days.

Receipts and invoices

Stripe emails a receipt automatically at purchase. You can also get receipts and invoices any time from your secure billing page using Manage my subscription above, or ask us below and we will re-send it.

Contact us

Send us a message and we will reply by email, usually within one business day. You can also email {{ support_email }} directly.
