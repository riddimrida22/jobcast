Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

JobBreak ("we", "us") is operated by JobBreak Inc. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to give you a result.

What we collect

  • What you enter to get an assessment: job title, general location (ZIP or city and state), and optional details such as age band, years of experience, education, union status, pay band, and skills. We do not ask for your name, and we do not collect race, gender, or other protected characteristics.
  • Your resume (optional): if you upload one to auto-fill the form, it is read in memory to extract a job title and skills, then discarded. We do not store your resume.
  • Email address (optional): only if you ask us to notify you when your outlook changes, or if you subscribe.
  • Payment information: subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Basic technical data: a session cookie to keep you signed in and to remember an active subscription, plus standard server logs.

How we use it

  • To produce your automation-exposure assessment and, on paid plans, your AI-researched plan.
  • To email you outlook alerts, only if you opted in.
  • To process and manage subscriptions.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share only with the service providers needed to run JobBreak: Stripe (payments), Anthropic (to generate the AI-written plan on paid plans), and our hosting provider. Each processes data only to provide its service.

Retention

Form inputs are used to generate your result and are not kept in a personal profile. Resumes are not stored. An email address you give us is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Subscription records are kept as required for billing and tax.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time, and you can ask us to delete your email and any subscription record by contacting us. We use no third-party advertising trackers.

Children

JobBreak is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the last change. Questions or requests: privacy@jobbreak.app.
