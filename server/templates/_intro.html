Horses → cars

In 1900, cities ran on horses — more than 100,000 in New York alone, plus the stable hands, farriers, and carriage makers who served them. Within about 20 years, cars replaced nearly all of it. Those jobs went extinct — but new ones appeared, and the people who saw it coming moved first.
