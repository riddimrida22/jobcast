How this score is calculated

  1. Map {{ r.occupation.title }} to its standard O*NET task list ({{ r.tasks|length }} core tasks).
  2. Score each task for AI/software exposure (Eloundou et al. 2023) and physical/robotics exposure (an O*NET work-context proxy), each 0 to 100.
  3. The two axis scores are the task averages: AI {{ r.exposure.ai_software }}, robotics {{ r.exposure.physical_robotics }}.
  4. The timeline comes from the higher axis ({{ r.base_exposure }}), shifted for local demand ({{ r.timeline.local_demand_trend }}){% if r.timeline.union_protected %}, union coverage{% endif %}, and seniority. It is read against your remaining working years.
  5. Ranges are about ±{{ r.exposure_range.half_width }} points. A backtest against real BLS employment change shows the risk ranking holds in aggregate, but individual outcomes are predicted weakly, so results are ranges, never a flat verdict.
