More detail on the method

How this is computed. Every task is scored for AI exposure (task-level language-model capability, after Eloundou et al.) and for robotics (an O*NET physical work-context proxy). Your exposure score is the higher of the two across all tasks, {{ r.base_exposure|round|int }} of 100 (range {{ r.exposure_range.ai_software[0]|round|int }} to {{ r.exposure_range.ai_software[1]|round|int }}); your resilience level bands where that sits across all occupations, from 1 (extinct) to 10 (safe).
