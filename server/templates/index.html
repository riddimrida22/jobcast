Straight answers

Is this just fear-mongering?

No. We show ranges and a timeline from published research, never a flat "your job is gone." The point is to help you act early, while you still have options, not to scare you.

Where do the numbers come from?

O*NET task data, BLS pay and projections, and peer-reviewed AI-exposure research. Every score links to its sources on the How it works page. No score is invented by an AI.

Does a high score mean I will lose my job?

No. Exposure measures how automatable your tasks are, not whether you will be replaced. Many exposed roles change rather than disappear, and the shift usually takes years, not months.

Is my data stored?

Your resume is read in your browser session to auto-fill the form and is never stored. We only keep an email address if you ask us to notify you when your outlook changes.

What should I do with my result?

Open the "What to do" plan: concrete, prioritized steps. Paid plans turn it into a researched, cited action plan specific to your job and location.
