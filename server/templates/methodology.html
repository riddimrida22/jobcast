How it works

Every number comes from a transparent model built on published data. A language model is never used to produce a score; it is used only to explain and to write the paid mitigation plan.

The model, in order

  1. Map your job to a standard occupation and its task list.
  2. Score each task on two axes: AI / software exposure and physical / robotics exposure. The occupation's scores are the task averages, shown 0 to 100.
  3. Adjust for seniority, which shifts your task mix toward less-exposed oversight work.
  4. Band the timeline from the exposure, then shift it for local demand and for union or other negotiated protection, and read it against your remaining working years.
  5. Distil the key drivers: the few factors that move your result the most.
  6. Suggest lower-risk adjacent roles; the paid tiers add a ranked plan.

Results are ranges with a stated confidence, never a single verdict. Every number decomposes to the tasks and sources behind it.

What we never use

Gender, race, disability, and other protected characteristics are never inputs to the score. Age is used only to work out your remaining working years, never to raise or lower exposure.
