Before we start, how the score works

One number, 0 to 100

It is how much of a job's everyday tasks AI and automation can already do. The higher the number, the more of the work a machine can take on.

0, safe
100, exposed
  • Built from real task data (O*NET) and peer-reviewed AI research, not opinion
  • It is exposure, not a verdict, and comes with a timeline for when it matters
  • Every score links to its sources
