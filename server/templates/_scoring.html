How we answer that

You're about to look through real careers, and each one carries a score.

What the score means

It runs from 0 to 100: how much of the work AI (artificial intelligence) and automation can already do. The higher the number, the more of the job a machine can take on.

0, safe
100, exposed
  • Built from real task data (O*NET) and peer-reviewed AI research, not opinion
  • It is exposure, not a verdict, and comes with a timeline for when it matters
  • Every score links to its sources
