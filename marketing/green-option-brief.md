# Creator brief angle: "The AI-safe career you already qualify for"

The green option is JobBreak's paid payoff and the primary ad hook. Fear gets the click (your
risk score and when it is coming); hope gets the purchase and the share (a concrete, lower-risk
career you can already reach with the skills you have). Lead the creative with the hope.

## The hook, in one line
"Find the AI-safe career you already qualify for." Not "your job is doomed" - the safer move you
can make today, with the numbers behind it.

## The 15-second UGC beat (TikTok)
1. Hook (0-3s): "AI is coming for my job - so I checked how bad it actually is." Show the score
   reveal (e.g. 78/100).
2. Turn (3-8s): "But here's the part that surprised me." Show the green card: a safer role, the
   big "you already qualify for 4 of 6 core skills," and the risk dropping 78 -> 22.
3. Payoff (8-13s): "Same skills. Way lower risk. It even showed the two things to learn and the
   typical pay." Point at the skills-to-add chips and the pay figure.
4. CTA (13-15s): "Check yours - link in bio." Keep it calm and practical, never doom.

## What makes it credible (say it plainly)
- The match is from real data: O*NET related-occupation and task data crossed with the model's
  automation-exposure scores, matched to the skills you enter. Not an AI guessing.
- Framed as ranges for planning, never a verdict. This is about livelihoods - no false certainty.

## Paywall copy (shipped)
- Headline: "Find the AI-safe career you already qualify for."
- Sub: "You've seen your risk. Now unlock your safer move: a lower-risk career you can reach with
  the skills you already have, plus every exposed task, your timeline, and a step-by-step plan.
  One-time $4.99."
- First unlock bullet leads with the green option, not the task breakdown.

## Angles to test
- "You're already 80% qualified for a safer job and didn't know it."
- "The one career move that cuts your AI risk in half - using skills you already have."
- "I was scared AI would take my job. Then it showed me the exit."
- Occupation-specific cuts for the highest-risk, highest-volume jobs (data entry, customer
  service, bookkeeping, telemarketing, retail) where the green card lands hardest.

## Guardrails
- Never promise a job or an income. "Typical pay" is a national median, shown as such.
- Keep the tone practical and kind. The audience is anxious about their livelihood; the product
  is the calm, concrete next step, not the panic.
